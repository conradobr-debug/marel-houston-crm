Privacy Notice

The Marel Houston CRM is an internal system used to manage sales opportunities, projects, professional relationships, appointments, and service activity.

Information handled

The CRM may store contact information, project addresses, project requirements, professional relationships, sales activity, appointments, notes, documents, and service records.

Access and security

Access is limited to authorized users. The production environment must use the independent Marel Houston authentication, database, storage, and integration configuration.

Communication channels

Customer activity may be recorded from email, phone calls, website forms, showroom visits, referrals, professional introductions, and events.
